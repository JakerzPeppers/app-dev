# 📺 My Favorite Series: *Monogatari Series*

![Monogatari Banner](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/06/Monogatari_series_logo.png)

## 🌸 Overview
The **Monogatari Series** is a Japanese light novel series written by *Nisio Isin* and illustrated by *Vofan*.  
It follows *Koyomi Araragi*, a high school student who survives a vampire attack and finds himself helping various girls with supernatural problems.

---

## 🧛 Main Themes
- **Supernatural** — focuses on oddities, spirits, and mysterious phenomena  
- **Psychological** — deep character introspection and emotional struggles  
- **Romance & Comedy** — witty dialogue and quirky relationships  

---

## 💖 My Favorite Characters
1. **Hitagi Senjougahara** — Sharp-tongued and confident  
2. **Koyomi Araragi** — The main protagonist who always helps others  
3. **Shinobu Oshino** — The vampire companion with a mysterious past

## 💭 Why I Love It
> “It’s not just about monsters — it’s about the *oddities* inside people.”

The series mixes fast dialogue, deep symbolism, and beautiful art direction.  
Each arc feels like poetry — strange, emotional, and unforgettable.
